MUKHYAMANTRI LADLI BEHNA YOJANA
GOVERNMENT OF MADHYA PRADESH
DEPARTMENT OF WOMEN AND CHILD DEVELOPMENT - OFFICIAL NOTIFICATION

1. SCHEME OBJECTIVES
The Mukhyamantri Ladli Behna Yojana was formulated by the Government of Madhya Pradesh to foster health, nutritional well-being, economic self-reliance, and familial empowerment for women residing in the state of Madhya Pradesh.

2. FINANCIAL BENEFIT
Eligible women receive an unconditional Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) of Rs 1,250 every month (totaling Rs 15,000 annually) directly deposited into their Aadhaar-linked personal bank accounts on the 10th day of each month.

3. ELIGIBILITY NORMS
- Domicile: The woman must be a local resident of the state of Madhya Pradesh.
- Gender and Marital Status: Woman must be female (married, widowed, divorced, or deserted).
- Age Criterion: Must have completed 21 years of age and must not have exceeded 60 years of age as on 1st January of the application year.

4. INELIGIBILITY / EXCLUSION CRITERIA
A woman is ineligible if her family falls into any of the following categories:
- Combined annual family income exceeds Rs 2,50,000.
- Any family member is an income tax payer.
- Any family member is a regular/contractual employee or pensioner of Central/State Government or public undertaking.
- Family owns more than 5 acres (approx. 2 hectares) of agricultural land.
- Family owns any four-wheeler motor vehicle (excluding tractor used for farming).
- Beneficiary is already receiving Rs 1,250 or more per month from any other government DBT scheme.

5. REQUIRED DOCUMENTS
- Samagra Family ID and Individual Member ID (with completed e-KYC).
- Aadhaar Card with active mobile linkage.
- Personal Bank Account linked with Aadhaar NPCI mapper for DBT.
- No physical paper certificate or income affidavit is required; verification is conducted via Samagra digital database.

6. APPLICATION PROCEDURE
Applications are accepted during camp sessions organized at every Gram Panchayat in rural areas and Ward offices in urban areas across Madhya Pradesh, or through designated CSC counters.
