POST-MATRIC SCHOLARSHIP SCHEME FOR SCHEDULED CASTE STUDENTS (PMS-SC)
MINISTRY OF SOCIAL JUSTICE AND EMPOWERMENT - GOVERNMENT OF INDIA
CENTRAL SECTOR SCHEME GUIDELINES

1. PURPOSE OF THE SCHEME
The objective of the scheme is to provide financial assistance to Scheduled Caste students studying at post-matriculation or post-secondary stage to enable them to complete their higher education.

2. VALUE OF SCHOLARSHIP
The scholarship comprises:
(a) Compulsory non-refundable fees (including tuition fee, library fee, examination fee, sports fee) paid by the institution.
(b) Academic allowance according to course categories:
- Group 1 (Degree & Post Graduate in Medicine, Engineering, Technology, Management): Rs 13,500 per annum for hostellers; Rs 7,000 for day scholars.
- Group 2 (Professional Courses, Pharmacy, Nursing, Law): Rs 9,500 per annum for hostellers; Rs 6,500 for day scholars.
- Group 3 (General Graduate and Post Graduate courses in Arts, Science, Commerce): Rs 6,000 for hostellers; Rs 3,000 for day scholars.
- Group 4 (Class 11, Class 12, ITI courses, 3-year Polytechnic Diplomas): Rs 4,000 for hostellers; Rs 2,500 for day scholars.

3. ELIGIBILITY CONDITIONS
- Nationality: Applicant must be an Indian citizen.
- Category: Must belong to a notified Scheduled Caste (SC) of the state/UT to which the applicant belongs.
- Student Enrolment: Must have completed matriculation or secondary examination (Class 10) and be enrolled in a recognized post-secondary institution.
- Income Ceiling: Total family income from all sources shall not exceed Rs 2,50,000 per annum.
- One scholarship only: A student cannot hold more than one government scholarship at the same time.

4. REQUIRED DOCUMENTS
- Digitally signed Caste Certificate issued by an officer not below the rank of Tehsildar / SDO.
- Current Annual Family Income Certificate.
- Class 10 / Matriculation passing marksheet and previous qualifying degree certificate.
- Bonafide Student Certificate and Fee Receipt from current College/Institute.
- Aadhaar Card and active Bank Passbook seeded with NPCI Aadhaar mapper.

5. APPLICATION PROCEDURE
Applications are invited annually on the National Scholarship Portal (NSP) at https://scholarships.gov.in/. Students must register with an OTR (One Time Registration) and submit institutional verification.
