AYUSHMAN BHARAT - PRADHAN MANTRI JAN AROGYA YOJANA (AB-PMJAY)
NATIONAL HEALTH AUTHORITY (NHA) - MINISTRY OF HEALTH & FAMILY WELFARE
OFFICIAL BENEFICIARY & OPERATIONAL GUIDELINES

1. SCHEME OVERVIEW
Ayushman Bharat PM-JAY is the flagship public health assurance scheme of the Government of India. It provides health assurance cover of Rs 5 Lakh per family per year for secondary and tertiary care hospitalization to over 12 crore poor and vulnerable families (approximately 55 crore beneficiaries) representing the bottom 40% of the Indian population.

2. BENEFITS AND COVERAGE
- Rs 5,00,000 annual family floater cover.
- No cap on family size, age, or gender.
- Covers medical and surgical treatment across 1,949 packages in 27 medical specialties.
- Completely cashless and paperless at all empanelled public and private hospitals across India.
- Covers pre-existing diseases from day one.
- 3 days of pre-hospitalization and 15 days of post-hospitalization costs (including diagnostics, medications, and transport allowance) are covered.

3. ELIGIBILITY AND TARGET BENEFICIARIES
Rural Beneficiary Criteria (SECC 2011 Deprivation Indicators):
- D1: Only one room with kuccha walls and kuccha roof.
- D2: No adult member between age 16 and 59.
- D3: Female-headed households with no adult male member between 16 and 59.
- D4: Disabled member and no able-bodied adult member.
- D5: SC/ST households.
- D7: Landless households deriving major part of their income from manual casual labour.
- Automatically included: Destitute families, living on alms, manual scavengers, primitive tribal groups.

Urban Beneficiary Criteria (11 Occupational Categories):
- Ragpickers, Beggars, Domestic workers.
- Street vendors, Cobblers, Hawkers, other service providers working on streets.
- Construction workers, Plumbers, Masons, Labourers, Painters, Welders, Security guards.
- Sweepers, Sanitation workers, Mali.
- Transport workers, Drivers, Conductors, Helpers to drivers, Cart pullers, Rickshaw pullers.
- Shop workers, Assistants, Peons in small establishments, Attendants, Delivery boys.
- Mechanics, Electricians, Repair workers, Assemblers.
- Washers, Chowkidars, Artisans, Tailors, Handicraft workers.

4. REQUIRED IDENTIFICATION DOCUMENTS
- Aadhaar Card or any authorized Government Photo Identity Card.
- Ration Card (NFSA or state Antyodaya Anna Yojana / Priority Household card).
- Mobile number for OTP verification.

5. HOW TO AVAIL BENEFITS
- Eligible individuals do not need to enroll through an open registration form. They must verify eligibility at beneficiary.nha.gov.in.
- Visit any empanelled public or private hospital and contact the Ayushman Mitra at the dedicated help desk.
- Complete on-the-spot e-KYC to generate the Ayushman Golden Card.
- Avail cashless treatments immediately.
