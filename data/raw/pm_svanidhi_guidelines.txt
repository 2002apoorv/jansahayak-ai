PM STREET VENDOR'S ATMANIRBHAR NIDHI (PM SVANIDHI)
MINISTRY OF HOUSING AND URBAN AFFAIRS - GOVERNMENT OF INDIA
OPERATIONAL GUIDELINES FOR WORKING CAPITAL LOANS TO STREET VENDORS

1. SCHEME OBJECTIVES
PM SVANidhi is a special micro-credit facility launched to provide affordable working capital loans to urban street vendors, hawkers, and thelewalas who were adversely affected by economic disruptions, helping them re-commence their livelihood activities.

2. LOAN TRANCHES AND INCENTIVES
- 1st Tranche: Collateral-free working capital loan of up to Rs 10,000 with a repayment tenure of 1 year.
- 2nd Tranche: Upon timely or early repayment of the first loan, vendors are eligible for an enhanced working capital loan of up to Rs 20,000.
- 3rd Tranche: Vendors who repay the 2nd tranche successfully qualify for up to Rs 50,000.
- Interest Subsidy: An interest subsidy of 7% per annum is credited directly into the borrower's bank account on a quarterly basis for regular repayments.
- Digital Cashback: Incentive of up to Rs 100 per month (Rs 1,200 annually) for conducting transactions through digital QR payment modes.

3. ELIGIBILITY NORMS
- The scheme is available to all street vendors engaged in vending in urban areas.
- Eligible vendors must possess a Certificate of Vending (CoV) or Identity Card issued by Urban Local Bodies (ULBs) / Town Vending Committees (TVCs).
- Vendors who have been left out of the ULB identification survey can obtain a Letter of Recommendation (LoR) from the ULB or Town Vending Committee.
- Must be at least 18 years of age.

4. REQUIRED DOCUMENTS
- Certificate of Vending (CoV) or Identity Card or Letter of Recommendation (LoR).
- Aadhaar Card linked with active mobile number.
- Bank Account details (saving account passbook).

5. APPLICATION PROCESS
Vendors can apply directly on https://pmsvanidhi.mohua.gov.in/ or through Common Service Centres (CSCs), municipal offices, or banking correspondents.
