PRADHAN MANTRI AWAS YOJANA - URBAN (PMAY-U)
HOUSING FOR ALL MISSION
MINISTRY OF HOUSING AND URBAN AFFAIRS - GOVERNMENT OF INDIA

1. OBJECTIVES AND OVERVIEW
The Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana (Urban) was initiated to address urban housing shortage among Economically Weaker Sections (EWS) and Lower Income Groups (LIG), including slum dwellers, by ensuring a pucca house to all eligible urban households.

2. VERTICALS OF THE SCHEME
The mission operates through four main verticals:
(a) In-situ Slum Rehabilitation (ISSR) using land as a resource with private participation.
(b) Credit Linked Subsidy Scheme (CLSS) providing upfront interest subsidy on home loans.
(c) Affordable Housing in Partnership (AHP) with public or private sectors.
(d) Beneficiary-led individual house construction or enhancement (BLC) providing direct central assistance of Rs 1.50 Lakh.

3. ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA
- Household Definition: A beneficiary family comprises husband, wife, unmarried sons, and/or unmarried daughters.
- Pucca House Restriction: The beneficiary family must NOT own a pucca (all-weather permanent concrete) house anywhere in India.
- Income Ceilings:
  * Economically Weaker Section (EWS): Household annual income up to Rs 3,00,000.
  * Low Income Group (LIG): Household annual income between Rs 3,00,001 and Rs 6,00,000.
  * Middle Income Group I (MIG-I): Annual income between Rs 6,00,001 and Rs 12,00,000.
  * Middle Income Group II (MIG-II): Annual income between Rs 12,00,001 and Rs 18,00,000.
- Mandatory Female Ownership: The house must be registered in the name of the female head of the family or in joint ownership with male head (except in cases of single males/widowers).

4. REQUIRED DOCUMENTS
- Aadhaar number of all family members.
- Valid Income Certificate or Form 16 issued by employer / Tehsildar.
- Land title deed / Patta for Beneficiary Led Construction (BLC).
- Bank account details (Account number, IFSC code).
- Self-declaration affidavit stating no pucca house is owned anywhere in India.

5. APPLICATION PROCESS
Citizens can self-apply at https://pmaymis.gov.in/ under 'Citizen Assessment' or through local municipal corporation offices or Common Service Centres (CSCs).
