PRADHAN MANTRI MUDRA YOJANA (PMMY)
MICRO UNITS DEVELOPMENT & REFINANCE AGENCY (MUDRA)
DEPARTMENT OF FINANCIAL SERVICES - MINISTRY OF FINANCE

1. MISSION OBJECTIVE
Pradhan Mantri Mudra Yojana (PMMY) aims to 'fund the unfunded' by extending collateral-free institutional credit to non-corporate, non-farm small and micro enterprises. The loans are provided by Commercial Banks, Regional Rural Banks (RRBs), Small Finance Banks (SFBs), MFIs, and NBFCs.

2. LOAN PRODUCT CATEGORIES
MUDRA loans are structured into three distinct categories based on enterprise development stage:
(a) Shishu: Covering loans up to Rs 50,000 for early-stage micro entrepreneurs.
(b) Kishore: Covering loans from Rs 50,001 up to Rs 5,00,000 for purchasing machinery, tools, and inventory.
(c) Tarun: Covering loans from Rs 5,00,001 up to Rs 10,00,000 for established small enterprises scaling operations (limit enhanced up to Rs 20 Lakh in select sectors).

3. KEY FEATURES
- Zero Collateral Requirement: No asset mortgaging is required from the borrower.
- Mudra Card: A RuPay debit card providing working capital credit with minimal interest cost on utilized balance.
- Processing Fee: Zero processing fee for Shishu and Kishore loans.

4. ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA
- Age: Applicant must be an Indian citizen of at least 18 years of age.
- Purpose: Must be for an income-generating micro business in manufacturing, trading, or service sectors (e.g., grocery shop, salon, repair workshop, artisanal handicraft, textile garment tailoring, small transport vehicle operation).
- Credit History: Must not be a defaulter to any financial institution.

5. REQUIRED DOCUMENTS
- Proof of Identity (Aadhaar Card, Voter ID, PAN Card, Driving License).
- Proof of Residence (Electricity bill, Ration card, Aadhaar).
- Quotation of machinery, tools, or items to be purchased.
- Business registration / Udyam certificate (if available).
- Bank statement for the last 6 months.

6. HOW TO APPLY
Borrowers can apply online through the portal https://www.udyamimitra.in/ or directly visit any commercial bank or MFI branch with completed application form and project estimate.
