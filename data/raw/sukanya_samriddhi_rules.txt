SUKANYA SAMRIDDHI ACCOUNT (SSA) RULES
DEPARTMENT OF POSTS / MINISTRY OF FINANCE - GOVERNMENT OF INDIA
BETI BACHAO BETI PADHAO MISSION

1. OVERVIEW
Sukanya Samriddhi Yojana (SSY) is a government-backed small savings scheme designed specifically for the financial security and higher education empowerment of female children in India.

2. KEY RULES AND ATTRIBUTES
- Opening of Account: Can be opened by natural parents or legal guardians in the name of a girl child from her birth until she attains the age of 10 years.
- Number of Accounts: Only one account can be opened per girl child. A maximum of two accounts are permitted per family (with exception for twins/triplets as supported by medical certificate).
- Minimum and Maximum Deposit: Minimum deposit of Rs 250 per financial year; maximum limit is Rs 1,50,000 per financial year.
- Interest Rate: Compounded annually with rates fixed quarterly by the Ministry of Finance (currently ~8.2% p.a.).
- Tax Exemption: Eligible for deduction under Section 80C of the Income Tax Act, 1961. The interest earned and the final maturity proceeds are completely tax-exempt (EEE status).
- Operation: Account is operated by guardian till girl child attains 18 years of age.
- Partial Withdrawal: Up to 50% of the balance at the end of preceding financial year can be withdrawn for higher education expenses after the girl turns 18 or passes Class 10.
- Maturity: The account matures after completion of 21 years from the date of opening or at the time of marriage of the girl child after attaining 18 years.

3. REQUIRED DOCUMENTS
- Birth Certificate of the girl child issued by competent municipal authority.
- Identity and Address proof of the parent or legal guardian (Aadhaar, Passport, Voter ID).
- Photographs of guardian and child.

4. APPLICATION PROCESS
Form-1 can be submitted at any Post Office or designated branches of authorized public and private commercial banks.
